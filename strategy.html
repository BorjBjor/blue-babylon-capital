How the trading approach is assessed

Methodology and risk considerations

Signals & execution

Each program follows a systematic decision process. The precise entry, exit and position-management rules will be documented after confirmation, without disclosing proprietary parameters.

Exposure & sizing

The intended common risk-setting framework does not mean equal volatility or drawdowns. CFD contract specifications, margin requirements and account equity can change effective exposure.

What can go wrong?

Whipsaws, gaps, fast markets, slippage, spreads, financing charges and prolonged unfavorable trends can all affect outcomes. Crypto markets may also exhibit extreme volatility.
