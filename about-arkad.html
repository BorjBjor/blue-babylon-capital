THE INVESTMENT PHILOSOPHY

Markets change.
The discipline remains.

01

Follow price, not predictions

Rules respond to market movements rather than relying on a single economic forecast or discretionary opinion.

02

Manage risk before returns

Position size, volatility, correlation and the possibility of extended drawdowns are central to strategy design.

03

Accept imperfect conditions

Trend strategies can struggle in sideways markets. Losses and false signals are part of systematic trading.
