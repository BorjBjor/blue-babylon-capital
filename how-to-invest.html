Five steps. One clear process.

The steps describe the intended workflow, not a guarantee that a particular account, product or strategy is available in your country.

02

Check eligibility and open an AvaTrade CFD account

Confirm directly with AvaTrade that CFD trading and DupliTrade access are offered to residents of your country. If eligible, complete account registration, identity verification and any suitability assessment required by the broker.

Ava Futures is a separate service and is not the account type described here.

03

Fund your own account

The suggested starting capital is USD 10,000 per strategy, subject to confirmation of position sizing and the strategy's actual capital requirements. This is not a broker minimum or a promise that losses will be limited to a particular amount. Your funds remain with the broker, not AlgoLodge.

04

Connect DupliTrade and select a strategy

Follow the current connection instructions supplied by AvaTrade and DupliTrade. Check whether the chosen AlgoLodge strategy is listed and available for your account. Review the copying settings, trade size, leverage and available margin before activation.

Do not assume that a strategy's trades or returns will match another account exactly.

05

Activate, monitor and manage risk

Once your account and connection are approved, you may activate copying according to the provider's instructions. Monitor open positions, margin, financing charges, execution differences and drawdowns. Understand how to pause or disconnect copying, including the treatment of existing positions.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Questions worth asking.

Is USD 10,000 the minimum deposit required by AvaTrade?

No. It is AlgoLodge's provisional suggested capital per strategy, not an assertion about the broker's minimum. Confirm broker requirements and strategy sizing before funding.

Can I follow more than one strategy?

Potentially, subject to availability, margin requirements and platform rules. Multiple strategies can create overlapping exposures. USD 10,000 per strategy should not be interpreted as an automatically sufficient amount for a combined portfolio.

Will my account reproduce the published performance?

Not necessarily. CFD spreads, financing costs, leverage, execution timing, slippage, position sizing and cash flows can all affect your result. Historical figures will be clearly identified by source and methodology when finalized.

Is this available in my country?

Availability varies by country and entity. The website being available in your language does not mean the broker or copy-trading service can accept you. Confirm eligibility directly with the providers.

How do I stop copying trades?

Consult DupliTrade and AvaTrade instructions before activation. Stopping future copying may not automatically close existing CFD positions, which can continue to incur risk and costs.

IMPORTANT RISK INFORMATION

CFD trading involves substantial risk.

CFDs are leveraged products and can cause rapid losses. Copy trading does not remove market, leverage, liquidity, execution or operational risk. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. Broker-specific regulatory risk disclosures, including any mandatory retail loss percentage, must be confirmed and displayed before public marketing.
