WHY ALTERNATIVE STRATEGIES?

A complement to equities.
Not another stock portfolio.

Traditional portfolios often rely heavily on rising equity markets. Systematic trading can pursue opportunities in other markets and may take different positions as conditions change. Its purpose here is broader diversification—not a promise of protection or positive returns when stocks fall.

Actual diversification depends on correlations, exposures and market conditions. These strategies can lose money at the same time as stocks, and their historical behavior must be assessed before making diversification claims.
