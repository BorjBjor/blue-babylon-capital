Viktig CFD-varning

CFDs are complex leveraged products. They can cause rapid and substantial losses. Copy trading does not eliminate risk, and it may produce different trades or results across accounts. Do not trade with funds you cannot afford to lose.

Handelsrisker och operativa risker

Leverage magnifies price changes. Slippage, spreads, gaps, margin close-outs, overnight financing, liquidity, platform outages, strategy interruptions and order-copying delays may affect results. Crypto-related CFDs can be especially volatile.

Risker med kopieringshandel

Copied orders can differ in timing, position size, margin use and execution price. Pausing copying may not automatically close existing positions. Multiple strategies may have correlated exposures.
